How NOTIFY works from Moodle to your phone

NOTIFY is intended to help students keep up with academic activity on Moodle. This guide explains how a student accesses the service, what the homepage sections are for, and how new Moodle information is expected to become a phone notification.

Accessing a NOTIFY account

Students use a NOTIFY account to manage the service and connect their Moodle account.

LOGIN

For a student who already has an account

The student opens the NOTIFY website and selects Log In. They enter the email address and password they used when registering for NOTIFY. After the details have been checked successfully, the student is taken to their account homepage, where they can view their notification settings and the Moodle account connected to their profile.

SIGN UP

For a student who does not have an account

The student selects Sign Up on the NOTIFY website and completes the registration details requested by the service, such as their name and email address. They create a password and complete any required email verification. Once registration is complete, they can use the new email address and password to log in to NOTIFY and continue with the Moodle connection process.

Information: A NOTIFY account is separate from a Moodle account. A real version should never ask students to share their Moodle password directly with an untrusted service; the Moodle connection should use an approved, secure authorization method.

About the programme

This programme was created to help describe new information uploaded on the UCU Moodle website. Students can miss coursework marks or important academic updates when they do not notice newly uploaded materials. NOTIFY is an idea for bringing updates from Moodle to students' notification panels, making important course information easier to notice among everyday social media notifications.

Current situation

UCU students currently rely on manually checking Moodle to find new assignments and deadlines, which makes it easy to miss important updates.

Our Proposed Solutions

UCU Notify is a proposed notification system designed to help students stay informed about newly uploaded assignments and upcoming deadlines. It works alongside Moodle to improve communication between lecturers and students.

How We Solve the Problem

1. Instant Assignment Notifications

Students receive notifications whenever a new assignment is uploaded by their lecturers. This helps students know about new coursework without constantly checking Moodle.

2. Automatic Deadline Reminders

The system reminds students about upcoming assignment deadlines. Reminders can be sent 24 hours and 1 hour before submission time to reduce late submissions.

3. Centralized Assignment Dashboard

All assignments are displayed in one place. Students can view assignment titles, courses, submission dates and their completion status without searching through different Moodle pages.

4. Course-Based Notifications

Students receive relevant assignment updates according to the courses they are enrolled in. This reduces unnecessary notifications and keeps information organized.

5. Assignment Tracking

Students can mark assignments as completed and identify pending or overdue work. This helps them organize their academic tasks and manage their time better.

6. Mobile-Friendly Access

The application is designed to work on smartphones and computers, allowing students to access assignment information wherever they are.

Expected Benefits

  • Reduced chances of missing assignments.
  • Improved student time management.
  • Fewer late assignment submissions.
  • Better communication between lecturers and students.
  • Easier access to academic updates.
  • Improved awareness of coursework deadlines.
