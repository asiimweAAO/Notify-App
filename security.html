How Your Moodle Account is Linked

Notify connects to your institutional Moodle instance using official Web Service API Tokens or OAuth2 SSO. We never store your raw Moodle password in clear text.

RECOMMENDED Password Safety
Password Requirements & Habits
  • Length & Complexity: Use at least 12 characters combining uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Avoid Re-use: Never use your Moodle or university password for third-party websites or personal social media.
  • Use a Password Manager: Store complex, unique passwords in a password manager (e.g., Bitwarden, 1Password).
THREAT WATCHLIST Stay Vigilant
What to Watch Out For
  • Phishing Alerts: Notify will never ask for your Moodle password via email, SMS, or Slack.
  • Shared Devices: Always log out of Notify and Moodle when using shared university library terminals.
  • Unrecognized Notifications: If you receive alerts for courses you aren't enrolled in, update your tokens immediately.
