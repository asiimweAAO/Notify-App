Our Proposed Solutions

These features address the challenge of students overlooking new coursework and important academic information on Moodle.

1. Instant Assignment Notifications

Students receive an alert when a new assignment is uploaded by a lecturer, reducing the need to check Moodle repeatedly.

2. Automatic Deadline Reminders

The system can send reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before an assignment is due to help students plan their work and reduce late submissions.

3. Centralized Assignment Dashboard

Assignments can be displayed in one place with their titles, courses, submission dates and completion status, making them easier to manage.

4. Course-Based Notifications

Students receive relevant updates according to the courses they are enrolled in, helping reduce unnecessary notifications and keep information organized.

5. Assignment Tracking

Students can mark assignments as completed and identify pending or overdue work, helping them organize academic tasks and manage time.

6. Mobile-Friendly Access

The proposed application is intended to work on smartphones and computers, allowing students to access assignment information wherever they are.

How UCU NOTIFY Works

A proposed process for detecting Moodle activity and bringing relevant information to students.

1

Access NOTIFY

Students visit the NOTIFY website or application and sign in or create an account.

2

Connect to Moodle

Students connect their account through the proposed Moodle access process.

3

Lecturer uploads content

A lecturer uploads an assignment, notes, marks or an announcement to Moodle.

4

Detect course activity

NOTIFY checks Moodle for new content and identifies the courses and students concerned.

5

Send student alerts

Students enrolled in the relevant course receive a notification on their phone or computer.

6

Update the dashboard

The new assignment or update appears on the student's dashboard with relevant details.

7

Remind before deadlines

For assignments, the system can send reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before the deadline.

About the Project

UCU NOTIFY is a proposed programme intended to make new information uploaded to the UCU Moodle website easier for students to notice. Students may overlook coursework marks, assignments or other academic updates when they do not regularly check Moodle. NOTIFY aims to bring relevant updates to students' notification panels, where they are easier to see among everyday notifications.

Current Project Status

NOTIFY is currently a project concept and is still a work in progress. The features and processes described on this page are proposed functions, not a claim that a complete Moodle integration is already operational.
