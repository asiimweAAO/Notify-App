How the programme will work

A simple proposed flow for notifying students about Moodle activity.

1

Create a NOTIFY account

Students sign up or log in to their NOTIFY account.

2

Connect Moodle

Students provide the appropriate access through a secure method that the project will define.

3

Check course activity

NOTIFY detects whether new content or unread updates are available.

4

Send a phone alert

New information is surfaced in the student’s phone notification panel.

About the programme

This programme was created to help describe new information uploaded on the UCU Moodle website. Students can miss coursework marks or important academic updates when they do not notice newly uploaded materials. NOTIFY is an idea for bringing updates from Moodle to students’ notification panels, making important course information easier to notice among everyday social media notifications.

Current situation

NOTIFY is currently a project concept. It is still a work in progress.
