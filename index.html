How NOTIFY works from Moodle to your phone

NOTIFY is intended to help students keep up with academic activity on Moodle. This guide explains how a student accesses the service and how new Moodle information is expected to become a phone notification.

Accessing a NOTIFY account

Students use a NOTIFY account to manage the service and connect their Moodle account.

LOGIN

For a student who already has an account

The student opens the NOTIFY website and selects Log In. They enter their registered email address and password. After successful authentication, the student is taken to their account homepage.

SIGN UP

For a student who does not have an account

The student selects Sign Up and completes the registration details requested by the service. They create a password and complete any required email verification.

Information: A NOTIFY account is separate from a Moodle account. A real version should use an approved, secure authorization method to connect Moodle.

About the programme

This programme was created to help students notice new information uploaded on the UCU Moodle website. Students can miss coursework marks or important academic updates when they do not notice newly uploaded materials. NOTIFY aims to bring updates from Moodle to students' notification panels.

Current situation

UCU students currently rely on manually checking Moodle to find new assignments and deadlines, which makes it easy to miss important updates.

Our Proposed Solutions

UCU Notify is a proposed notification system designed to help students stay informed about newly uploaded assignments and upcoming deadlines. It works alongside Moodle to improve communication between lecturers and students.

How We Solve the Problem

1. Instant Assignment Notifications

Students receive notifications whenever a new assignment is uploaded by their lecturers.

2. Automatic Deadline Reminders

The system reminds students about upcoming assignment deadlines to reduce late submissions.

3. Centralized Assignment Dashboard

All assignments are displayed in one place, including titles, courses, submission dates and completion status.

4. Course-Based Notifications

Students receive relevant assignment updates according to the courses they are enrolled in.

5. Assignment Tracking

Students can identify pending, completed or overdue work.

6. Mobile-Friendly Access

The application is designed to work on smartphones and computers.

Expected Benefits

  • Reduced chances of missing assignments.
  • Improved student time management.
  • Fewer late assignment submissions.
  • Better communication between lecturers and students.
  • Easier access to academic updates.
  • Improved awareness of coursework deadlines.
