Our Features

Notify helps students stay updated about assignments and important announcements.

Student Login

Students can create accounts and log in to access their notifications.

Notification Dashboard

Students can view all their notifications in one place.

New Assignment Alerts

Students receive alerts whenever new assignments are posted.

Deadline Reminders

Notify reminds students about upcoming assignment deadlines.

Read and Unread Notifications

Students can identify notifications they have read and those they have not read.

Assignment Status Tracking

Students can check the status of their assignments.

Course-Based Notifications

Students receive announcements related to their courses.

Moodle Assignment Links

Students can click links to open their assignments directly on Moodle.
