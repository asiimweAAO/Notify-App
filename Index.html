Our Proposed Solutions

UCU Notify is a proposed notification system designed to help students stay informed about newly uploaded assignments and upcoming deadlines. It works alongside Moodle to improve communication between lecturers and students.

How We Solve the Problem

😝

1. Instant Assignment Notifications

Students receive notifications whenever a new assignment is uploaded by their lecturers. This helps students know about new coursework without constantly checking Moodle.

⏰

2. Automatic Deadline Reminders

The system reminds students about upcoming assignment deadlines. Reminders can be sent 24 hours and 1 hour before submission time to reduce late submissions.

📚

3. Centralized Assignment Dashboard

All assignments are displayed in one place. Students can view assignment titles, courses, submission dates and their completion status without searching through different Moodle pages.

🎓

4. Course-Based Notifications

Students receive relevant assignment updates according to the courses they are enrolled in. This reduces unnecessary notifications and keeps information organized.

✅

5. Assignment Tracking

Students can mark assignments as completed and identify pending or overdue work. This helps them organize their academic tasks and manage their time better.

📱

6. Mobile-Friendly Access

The application is designed to work on smartphones and computers, allowing students to access assignment information wherever they are.

How UCU Notify Works

Step 1: A lecturer uploads an assignment to Moodle.
Step 2: The notification system detects the new assignment.
Step 3: Students enrolled in the relevant course receive an alert.
Step 4: The assignment appears on the student's dashboard.
Step 5: The system sends reminders as the deadline approaches.
